# Model Intelligence

The model inventory combines configured pricing with observed request count, success rate, latency, tokens per second, historical cost, context size, and estimated monthly cost at the current observed rate.

Models → Compare accepts two to five unique models. Price is labeled CONFIGURED, operational data OBSERVED, monthly cost ESTIMATED, and quality USER-SUPPLIED ONLY. The mandatory disclaimer states that cost comparison does not imply equivalent output quality.

## Evaluation evidence

TokenScope never invents quality scores. Users may submit an evaluation metric, score and maximum scale, sample size, source, application, workload, and notes. Normalized percentages are calculated only within the named metric. Evaluation creation and deletion are audited.

Quality evidence should come from representative, versioned test sets. Comparing unlike evaluation metrics or datasets is not scientifically valid; TokenScope displays their names and sources so users can judge comparability.
