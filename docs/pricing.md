# Pricing

TokenScope ships a small versioned registry for demonstration and offline operation. Prices represent cost per million tokens and are estimates, not guarantees. `PUT /api/v1/pricing/{model_id}` creates an organization-specific override. Overrides apply to future events; stored historical estimates are intentionally not rewritten.

Private, negotiated, and local-model prices should always be configured explicitly. Registry data includes its effective date, source, and verification date.
